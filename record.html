The only part that cannot be done late

Everything else in Access Lab can be caught up. The record cannot. It is also the clean-room evidence that each resource was made on my own time, with my own equipment, from public sources, which is what keeps it mine to give away.

Where the record actually lives. The canonical record is access-lab-record.md in this repository, append-only. The log on this page is a scratchpad kept in this browser only: it can be cleared by the browser, and it is not backed up. At the end of each Tuesday, press Copy as Markdown and paste the rows into the file.

The question

One dated paragraph a year, kept in question.md: what has the work found this year about how learning design decides who is included? Never revise an earlier year. Ten dated versions are a trajectory, and the 2027 version is the spine of the Leeds statement.

The rule for contributors

Every disabled contributor, reader or co-author is credited as they wish to be named and paid, with both settled in writing before anything is published. If someone declines payment, the record says they declined; it never says unpaid.

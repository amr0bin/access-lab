The dates set by somebody else, and the few set by me

Self-imposed deadlines are not deadlines, so this page keeps them apart. Verified means the date was read on the organisation's own page, on the day recorded. Expected means it is projected from a previous cycle and must be confirmed. Target means I set it, and it is the only kind allowed to move. Tuesdays are shaded.

What the postings, Leeds, a committee and a call for proposals all ask for, prepared once and refreshed each quarter, so an application is assembly rather than production.

    How this page is kept

    Dates live in calendar-data.js, one entry each, with a source and the day it was read. A projected date stays expected until the call is posted. A passed entry moves to the record rather than being deleted, because a list of what was applied for is itself evidence. The window is rewritten each November at the review.

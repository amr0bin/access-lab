The YearSeptember 2026 to August 2027

Four phases, one Tuesday at a time

What the Tuesdays carry each month, what runs outside them, and what is true at the end of the month if it went well. The current month is highlighted. The dated column is drawn from the calendar, so it stays in step with it.

About 45 working Tuesdays before the MSc, at 7.5 hours: about 340 hours. The plans fit only because three things are sequenced: the corpus finishes in MSc year one, Fieldwork is fortnightly until the exam, and two of the four resources ship after the Leeds application.

The November review

First Tuesday of November, every year. One question per ledger: did it gain an entry? Then three: did the floor hold in the worst month; which row of the postings table moved; is the offer still described and priced as a specialism? The year's question paragraph is written the same day, and the calendar's window is rewritten.
